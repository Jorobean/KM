KEMS Mirrors is looking for a cofounder

November 25, 2026

KEMS Mirrors is seeking a business cofounder to help build the venture. This is a founding partnership with shared responsibility and decision-making, not a salaried job.

About KEMS Mirrors

KEMS Mirrors is developing a working hardware and software prototype designed to record someone while they naturally look at their reflection. Potential uses include practicing speeches, creating videos, rehearsing dance, and demonstrating skills.

About the partnership

The cofounder would help lead customer discovery, early sales, business development, fundraising, operations, and the path toward hardware commercialization. Relevant experience in consumer hardware, creator tools, or building an early-stage business would be valuable. The founders would share key decisions and define responsibilities together.

KEMS Mirrors is pre-revenue and has no funding or salary budget at this time. Meaningful equity can be discussed with the right partner; terms would be negotiated together, and no percentage has been set. A remote U.S.-based partnership is possible.

Interested candidates and referral sources can email to start a conversation.

What is KEMS?

KEMS (Kinetic Energy Management System) helps you capture everyday experiences, revisit meaningful moments, and turn them into something worth keeping or sharing. We’re building a more personal way to record, reflect, and create.

That focus brings together the moments you want to remember and the ideas you want to develop. An everyday experience can be worth returning to, whether you want to keep it for yourself or create something to share.
